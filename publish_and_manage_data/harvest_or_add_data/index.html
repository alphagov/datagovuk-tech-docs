Harvest or add data

You can publish datasets to the directory by:

  • using software to harvest datasets
  • adding datasets manually

Harvesting means using software, known as a ‘harvester’, to collect datasets from your website and publish them on the directory. Using the harvester lets you publish more than one dataset at once. You can also schedule harvests.

You must harvest Location/INSPIRE data.

If you’d like to showcase your data by making it part of the new curated data collections, you can express your interest through our feedback form.
