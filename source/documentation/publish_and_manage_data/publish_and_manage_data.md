# Publish and manage datasets

Central government, local authorities and public bodies can publish on the National Data Library directory.

If you have an existing account, you can log in to your account on [the data publishing service](https://ckan.publishing.service.gov.uk/user/login). When you log in, you will land on [your dashboard page](https://ckan.publishing.service.gov.uk/dashboard/datasets) which shows the datasets published from your account and the organisations (publishers) your account is associated with and allowed to publish for.

The National Data Library does not host datasets. Instead, you must first publish the dataset (or supporting document) on your organisation’s website, on GOV.UK or on your service's own hosting. You can then add the dataset to the directory, which will display a description and a link to the dataset so interested people can find it easily.

Find out how to:

* [get an account](get_and_manage_accounts/#get-an-account)
* [manage an existing account](get_and_manage_accounts/#add-or-remove-editors-and-admins)
* [harvest or add data](harvest_or_add_data/#harvest-or-add-data)
* [manage published data](managing_published_data/#managing-published-data)

If you’d like to showcase your data by making it part of the new [curated data collections](https://data.gov.uk), you can express your interest through our [feedback form](https://forms.office.com/e/9V26PNFQaR).
